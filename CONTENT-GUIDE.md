# Publishing articles

Every featured image must include accurate, descriptive `imageAlt` text. Describe the actual image naturally, without keyword stuffing. Other meaningful site images also require descriptive alt text; purely decorative images should use empty alt text.

When Aaron identifies a new post as evergreen, set `evergreen: true` in its article frontmatter. Otherwise retain the default `false` unless he approves a classification.

Evergreen means the core teaching remains useful independently of a news event, launch, or particular date. Hide its publication-date display on posts, cards, and category listings; keep its actual date for ordering, RSS, and truthful search metadata. Do not change dates or rewrite the body to make a post appear newer.
